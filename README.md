# HazardScout — Confined-Space Safety & Environmental Inspection Rover

> **Tagline:** Inspect First. Protect Lives.  
> **Project Type:** College-Level Engineering Hackathon Prototype  
> **Target Environment:** Industrial sewers, manholes, storm drainage conduits, underground utility vaults.

---

## 1. Project Overview

**HazardScout** is a specialized robotic reconnaissance prototype engineered to perform multi-channel atmospheric and environmental scouting inside hazardous confined spaces prior to human worker entry. 

Confined spaces such as sewers and utility conduits frequently harbor invisible, odorless, and lethal atmospheric hazards:
- **Oxygen Deficiency:** Displaced by heavier inert gases or microbial respiration.
- **Toxic Hydrogen Sulfide ($H_2S$):** Biogenic sewer gas causing rapid olfactory fatigue and sudden collapse.
- **Carbon Monoxide ($CO$):** Incomplete combustion byproduct trapped in subterranean pockets.
- **Flash Flooding & Standing Liquid:** Unseen sumps leading to equipment loss or drowning.

By sending an ESP32-governed inspection rover first, rescue and municipal maintenance teams assess real-time environmental telemetry, track collision-free conduit topology, and verify configurable safety margins before authorizing human entry permits.

> **DEMARCATION NOTICE:**  
> This application is an educational engineering simulation. Current live telemetry values and virtual threshold evaluations are generated by a deterministic local robotics simulation engine. It does not certify physical spaces for human occupancy without certified OSHA/NIOSH instrumentation.

## 3. Core Capabilities & Architecture

### A. Autonomous Reconnaissance & Rover Live View
- **Autonomous Reactive Navigation Agent:** Automatically navigates subterranean conduits via sequential target waypoints (Access Inflow -> West Chute -> Central Manifold -> Sump Basin -> Gas Plume Zone -> Outfall Chamber -> Ventilation Shaft -> Recovery Station).
- **Multi-Beam Ultrasonic Sonar Radar:** Real-time 5-ray acoustic scanning (-35° to +35°) dynamically sweeps for conduit walls and debris, biasing steering toward open clearances when obstacles approach within 38 cm.
- **Autonomous Critical Hazard Interlock & Retreat:** When hazardous gas or oxygen deficiency crosses critical safety thresholds, the autonomous agent immediately halts forward traversal, reverses away from the toxic plume, and engages autonomous Return-to-Base (RTH).
- **Autonomous & Manual Mode Toggle:** Allows switching between fully autonomous scouting and manual supervisor teleoperation at any moment.
- **2D Subterranean Sewer Canvas:** Renders perimeter concrete bulkheads, internal pipe conduits, masonry debris, standing water puddles, active waypoint beacons, and the live ultrasonic sonar beam fan.
- **Autonomous Return-to-Base (RTH):** Automatically retraces logged breadcrumb trails back to Manhole Access #1.

### B. Multi-Channel Environmental Transducer Array
- **Oxygen ($O_2$):** `% vol` (DFRobot SEN0322 electrochemical model proposed; nominal 19.5% - 21.0%).
- **Hydrogen Sulfide ($H_2S$):** `ppm` (Hypothetical gas-selective sensor channel; note: prototype MQ-135 is not gas-selective).
- **Carbon Monoxide ($CO$):** `ppm` (Hypothetical gas-selective electrochemical model).
- **Ambient Temperature:** `°C` (DFRobot SEN0334 Sensirion SHT31-F model).
- **Relative Humidity:** `% RH` (DFRobot SEN0334 Sensirion SHT31-F model).
- **Water Detection:** Binary `DRY / DETECTED` (FC-37 / YL-83 conductive ground probe).
- **Ultrasonic Distance:** `cm` (HC-SR04 acoustic time-of-flight raycaster).

### C. Live Telemetry & SCADA Oscilloscope
- Live scrolling line graph updating at 1 Hz with a rolling 120-sample bounded buffer.
- Selectable time windows: 30 Seconds, 1 Minute, 5 Minutes.
- Visual threshold warning and critical reference lines.
- Hover crosshairs with multi-channel tooltip inspector.

### D. Hazard Detection & Safety Semantics
- **State Transition Engine:** Trips alerts only on actual severity transitions, eliminating repetitive notification spam.
- **Strict Safety Semantics:**
  - Nominal readings display: `NO CONFIGURED THRESHOLDS EXCEEDED`.
  - Abnormal readings display: `HAZARD INDICATED — HUMAN ENTRY NOT AUTHORIZED BY THIS SIMULATION`.
- **Demo Scenarios:** Instantaneous hazard injection (Normal, Low Oxygen, Toxic Gas Leak, High Temperature, Water Intrusion, Multiple Hazards) + 1-Click `Restore Normal Scenario`.

### E. Hackathon Judge Presentation Demo Mode
- Dedicated 60–90 second guided presentation tour with on-screen step captions, stepper controls, and synchronized simulation transitions.

### F. Mission Debrief & CSV Export
- Comprehensive debrief showing mission duration, traversed distance, $O_2$ minimums, gas peaks, and hazard trip counts.
- 1-Click export to standard CSV with full metadata, sample timestamps, and simulated watermarks.

## 4. Hardware Implementation Roadmap (Physical ESP32 Prototype)

### Proposed Bill of Materials (BOM)
1. **ESP32 DevKit V1 (30-pin):** 240MHz dual-core Xtensa MCU with onboard 2.4GHz Wi-Fi / BLE.
2. **DFRobot SEN0322:** Calibrated I2C electrochemical galvanic oxygen sensor ($0 - 25\%\text{ vol}$).
3. **DFRobot SEN0334 (SHT31-F):** High-precision digital temperature and humidity probe.
4. **FC-37 / YL-83:** Sump water detection probe mounted on underside chassis.
5. **HC-SR04:** Front-facing ultrasonic distance transducer ($2\text{cm} - 400\text{cm}$).
6. **L298N Motor Driver:** Dual H-bridge driving 4x DC geared TT motors with rubber treads.
7. **LM2596 Buck Converter & 18650 Li-Ion Pack:** 7.4V battery pack stepped down to clean 5V logic.
